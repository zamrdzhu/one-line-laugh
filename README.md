One funny line of text, written for make.fun's "One Line, One Laugh" bounty.

## The line

> My plants died. I think they saw my search history.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

Two short sentences and one turn: the houseplants did not die of neglect, they died of what they witnessed. The first sentence sets up an ordinary failure every plant owner knows; the second gives it a motive nobody saw coming, and the laugh comes from the reader filling in the search history themselves — the joke never says what was in it, which is what makes it land. It is clear on first read, needs no explanation, and there is nothing left to trim. One line, 51 characters, well inside the 140-character gate.
